01 — THE MOTIVE

Biological Vitality meets Digital Performance

Verve addresses the nuanced relationship between how we work and our physiological stress levels. By actively monitoring application usage context and pairing it with real-time biometric sensors, Verve seeks to answer a critical question: when are you in a state of flow, and when are you suffering from digital burnout?

Our approach applies research in Human-Computer Interaction to surface metrics like Recovery Efficiency Score (RES) and Cognitive Divergence, allowing us to accurately predict mental load, context-switching penalties, and unoptimized workflows.

The Shadow & The Hub [ARCHITECTURE]

The Mobile Hub

React Native / Expo

Acts as the primary data aggregator and AI inference engine running directly on your phone.

  • Zero Cloud AI: Analyzes complex JSON workstation telemetry locally using llama.rn and Phi-4-mini-instruct.
  • Biometric Layer: Smoothly correlates Apple HealthKit / Google Health Connect data securely in the background.
  • High Performance: Uses expo-sqlite with sophisticated queries to generate clinical flow-state graphs.

The Shadow CLI

Go / macOS

A low-footprint background observer running native C hooks inside your workstation.

  • Silent Polling: Identifies the active window, churn rate, and idle periods without disrupting resources.
  • Outbox Pattern: Guarantees data durability during momentary sub-network disconnections.
  • Verve Restore: Detects hardware sleep states to pause data flow correctly and trigger a cognitive cooldown.
THE LOCAL BRIDGE

All communication uses mDNS (Zeroconf) to form a secure Local Area Network TCP socket. No telemetry leaves the physical perimeter of your local network environment. Devices are cryptographically paired and verified through a secure PIN handshake protocol.

02 — INITIATION

Setup & Installation Protocols

Follow these protocols to deploy Verve across your clinical environment. Our release architecture ensures secure, local-first integrity.

01

Deploy the Shadow CLI

Install the background sensor on your workstation. For macOS users, utilize our official Homebrew tap for automated updates.

brew tap gyandeeps/homebrew-tap && brew install verve-cli

Windows users: Download the latest binary from the standard release repository.

02

Acquire the Mobile Hub

Install the Mobile Hub on your iOS or Android device. Distributed via EAS (Expo Application Services) to ensure high-fidelity performance and background synchronization.

Download Verve Hub v0.1.0-preview
03

Secure Handshake

Initialize the CLI and open the Mobile Hub. Locate your workstation via the dynamic mDNS signature and verify the 6-digit cryptographic PIN displayed on your terminal.

04

Clinical Oversight

Once parity is achieved, the local synchronisation engine will begin correlating physiological telemetry. Your Focus Score and Recovery metrics will populate in real-time.
